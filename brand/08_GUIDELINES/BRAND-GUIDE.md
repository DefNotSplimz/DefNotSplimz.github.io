## Identity: Refined Precision

### Approved source of truth
The exact approved premium logo is:
`00_MASTER/DATUM-v3.1-premium-horizontal-transparent.png`

It is the **exact Refined Precision logo accepted in chat**, unchanged except for transparent-edge trimming/padding.

### Why there are two production systems

**PREMIUM MASTER**
- Exact approved metallic/shadow treatment.
- Transparent high-resolution PNG.
- Use on the website, social media, presentations, mockups and premium digital applications.
- The premium SVG wrapper embeds this exact PNG. It is for layout convenience; it is not falsely labelled as a true vector master.

**FLAT VECTOR**
- Derived from the exact approved logo silhouette.
- True SVG/PDF vector paths.
- White/orange, black/orange, mono white and mono black.
- Use for embroidery, screen printing, laser marking, vinyl, technical documents and large-format fabrication.

This prevents the brand from drifting while still providing genuinely scalable files where fabrication requires them.

### Logo rules
- Do not redraw DATUM in another font.
- Do not alter the D mark, A accent or datum triangle.
- Do not change proportions.
- Do not stretch/skew.
- Do not add new glows, bevels or effects.
- Do not use the premium raster for embroidery/engraving.

### Minimum sizes
- Horizontal digital: 150 px wide.
- Mark digital: 24 px.
- Horizontal print: 30 mm.
- Mark print: 8 mm.
- Embroidery: mark preferred below 90 mm.

### Clear space
Keep at least one orange-A-accent height of clear space around the complete logo.
