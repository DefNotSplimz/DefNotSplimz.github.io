# Datum Prototyping v3.1 — workwear

## Left chest
- Horizontal: 90–110 mm wide.
- Mark only: 30–38 mm wide.

## Back
- Horizontal: 260–310 mm wide.
- For smaller garments, use mark only.

## Sleeve
- Mark only: 38–46 mm wide.

## Production
Use the **flat vector SVG** files for embroidery, heat transfer, screen print and vinyl.
Do not embroider the photoreal premium metallic PNG.

Dark garments:
- white/orange or mono white.

Light garments:
- black/orange or mono black.

Datum Orange: #EA580C
